# Metrics and management review

**Status:** Draft. Do not set numeric targets until you have a baseline from real jobs.  
**Published ranges below are industry, not Shelley.**

---

## EMS metrics, translated

| What you tracked in EMS | What to track here |
| --- | --- |
| On-time delivery | Milestone hit rate **and** material availability (was the material staged when the crew needed it?) |
| Customer returns | Inspection first-pass (inverse of escapes), punchlist density, warranty callbacks |

Labor productivity is the dominant cost risk in electrical construction. Quality metrics that ignore labor vs estimate will not get PM attention.

---

## Start with these five

| # | Metric | How to compute | Where the data lives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Labor hours vs estimate | Actual hours ÷ estimated hours, by cost code, weekly per job | Vista | Margin. Feeds estimating. |
| 2 | Inspection first-pass rate | Inspections passed with no correction ÷ total inspections (AHJ + owner + GC) | F-08 | First-time quality. Closest to a yield metric. |
| 3 | Rework rate | Rework hours ÷ total labor hours **or** rework cost ÷ contract value | Vista cost codes + NCRs | Industry: average firms often 8–12% of contract value; top quartile ~3–5%. |
| 4 | Material availability | Crew-hours waiting on material, or stockout events per job | Wire Tracker + GF notes | Early win; app can produce this. |
| 5 | Days to closeout | Substantial completion date to closeout package complete | PM / F-07 | Typical mid-maturity ~30–45 days; stretch 21–30. |

## Add as the system matures

| Metric | Why | Published / typical range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estimating accuracy variance | Closes the loop from job cost back to the bid | Trend; lower is better |
| Bid-to-award win rate | Too high can mean underpricing; too low is targeting or price | Often cited ~30–40% healthy for electrical; below ~25% is a warning (service-oriented stats differ from hard-bid construction) |
| RFI cycle time | Slow answers stall crews | Construction: ~8–14 days typical; strong ~3–5 |
| Submittal turnaround | Late submittals delay buyout | Track separately from field performance |
| Change order recovery | Unbilled extra work is a process failure | Watch CO as % of original contract |
| Callback / warranty rate | Closest to a customer return | Service-side figures ~6% average / &lt;2% top; project warranty is job-specific |
| TRIR and EMR | Prequal | Owned by safety |
| Days sales outstanding | Cash | Owned by accounting |

---

## Management review (monthly, one page)

**Attendees:** President or designee, quality lead, at least one PM, accounting (job cost), safety as needed.

**Agenda (30–45 minutes):**

1. Five core metrics — trend, not a single month.  
2. Open NCRs older than [14] days.  
3. Failed inspections this month — pattern by crew or by scope?  
4. One labor variance story — what will estimating change?  
5. One improvement action with an owner and a date.  
6. Resources / training / license issues.

**Record:** Date, attendees, decisions, actions. Store with QM-001 records.

Do not add a metric unless someone will make a decision from it.
