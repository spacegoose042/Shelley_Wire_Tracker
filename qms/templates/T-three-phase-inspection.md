# T-3PH Three-phase inspection (federal / USACE-style)

**Shelley Electric, Inc.**  
Use this **in addition to** T-ITP when the contract requires Contractor Quality Control (CQC) — typical of USACE, NAVFAC, and some other federal work.

This is **not** required on a typical Wichita commercial job. Putting it in the starter pack means you are not learning it the night before a federal bid.

---

## The idea

For each **definable feature of work**, CQC uses three meetings/inspections:

| Phase | When | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Preparatory** | Before the feature starts | Confirm drawings, submittals, material, crew, equipment, and the ITP. Identify risks. |
| **Initial** | When the first representative portion is in place | Prove the crew understands quality requirements. Correct technique before the whole feature is installed. |
| **Follow-up** | Daily / ongoing until the feature is done | Confirm the same standard continues. |

The government or GC quality representative is often invited. Minutes are a contract record.

## Mapping to Shelley commercial jobs

On non-federal work you still do a lighter version without the government paperwork:

- Preparatory = GF / PM huddle before a new feature (row 1 of every ITP)  
- Initial = look at the first room / first panel before repeating 40 times  
- Follow-up = ordinary supervision + F-08 for AHJ
