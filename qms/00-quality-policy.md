Shelley Electric installs and maintains electrical, communications, and specialty systems. Our work is judged by whether it is safe, code-compliant, on schedule, and right the first time.

We will:

1. Meet the contract, the drawings, the specifications, and the adopted electrical code (NEC / NFPA 70 and local amendments).
2. Perform work to recognized workmanship standards, including applicable NECA National Electrical Installation Standards (NEIS).
3. Use licensed, qualified people — IBEW craft, supervising journeymen, and a qualifying master as required by MABCD and other jurisdictions.
4. Control material from purchase through receiving, storage, pull, and installation so that approved product is what gets installed, and so that material can be tied to the job number or work order.
5. Identify nonconforming work, correct it, and prevent recurrence.
6. Protect our people and the public. Safety requirements are never traded away to meet schedule.
7. Measure a small set of results — labor vs estimate, first-pass inspections, rework, material availability, and closeout — and improve them.
8. Review this policy at least annually.

Every employee and every subcontractor working under a Shelley contract is expected to stop work that is unsafe or clearly not in accordance with the approved drawings, specifications, or code, and to report it.

---

*This policy is posted in the shop and issued with the company quality manual. It is not a marketing statement. It is an operating commitment.*
