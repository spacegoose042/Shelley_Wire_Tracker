# QP-02 Purchasing and Vendors

**Shelley Electric, Inc.**  
**Revision:** A (Draft)

## 1. Purpose

Buy the specified, submittal-approved product, tied to the correct job, from vendors who can deliver.

## 2. Who

Purchasing / PM as assigned. Warehouse receiving uses QP-03.

## 3. Approved vendors

Maintain a simple approved vendor list (AVL): name, commodities (wire, gear, lighting, fire alarm, etc.), and status (approved / conditional / blocked).

New vendors: W-9, insurance if they deliver or subcontract, and one successful receipt or a PM justification. Specialty vendors (switchgear, fire alarm) may require manufacturer authorization.

Blocked vendors are not used until quality lead and purchasing agree.

## 4. Purchase orders

Every PO shall include:

- Vista job / work order number
- Part identity (catalog number, manufacturer, description) matching the approved submittal when one exists
- Quantity and unit
- Ship-to: warehouse **or** jobsite address
- Required date
- Reference to approved submittal number if applicable

Do not purchase “or equal” substitutions without written approval through the submittal / RFI process.

## 5. Subcontractors

Electrical or specialty subcontractors (for example fire alarm programming, high voltage testing) work under this QMS and the project ITP. Flow down license, insurance, and specification requirements on the subcontract.

## 6. Records

PO, AVL, submittals, vendor NCRs if product is wrong or late in a way that caused rework.
