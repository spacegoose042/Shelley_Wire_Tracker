# QP-05 Nonconformance and Corrective Action

**Shelley Electric, Inc.**  
**Revision:** A (Draft)  
**Related forms:** F-04, F-05

## 1. Purpose

Catch wrong material and wrong work, fix it, and stop repeats. This replaces the EMS “MRB + CAPA” habit with construction language.

## 2. What is a nonconformance

Examples:

- Failed AHJ / owner / GC inspection  
- Wrong catalog number installed or received  
- Damage, missing parts, wet gear  
- Work not per drawing or spec (including unapproved substitution)  
- Test failure  
- Missing required documentation (submittal not approved before install)

Punchlist items found at the end of the job are nonconformances too; they may be tracked on F-06 if they are numerous and minor. Systemic or repeat items still get F-04 / F-05.

## 3. Immediate action (F-04)

1. Identify and mark / segregate.  
2. Notify PM and GF.  
3. Disposition:
   - **Rework** to spec  
   - **Repair** (only if spec / engineer allows)  
   - **Return / scrap** material  
   - **Use-as-is** — **only** with written approval from the party who owns the spec (engineer / owner / GC as the contract requires). Shelley does not unilaterally accept use-as-is.  
4. Record who verified the fix.

## 4. Corrective action (F-05)

Open F-05 when:

- The same issue repeats  
- A failed inspection caused schedule damage or a safety issue  
- A customer complaint / callback  
- Management review requests it  

F-05 requires a simple root cause (5-why is enough) and an action that prevents recurrence (training, estimate change, vendor change, ITP hold point, Wire Tracker field, etc.).

Effectiveness: quality lead checks that the issue did not recur on the next similar job or the next 30 days, whichever fits.

## 5. Records

NCR log (spreadsheet is fine at first): number, job, date, type, disposition, closed date.
