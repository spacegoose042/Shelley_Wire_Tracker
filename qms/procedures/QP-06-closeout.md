# QP-06 Closeout

**Shelley Electric, Inc.**  
**Revision:** A (Draft)  
**Related forms:** F-06, F-07

## 1. Purpose

Finish the job so the owner can occupy, Shelley can bill retainage, and warranty is defined. Closeout is a quality process, not only an accounting event.

## 2. Punchlist (F-06)

- Log item, location, owner (Shelley / other trade), date found, date cleared, verified by.
- Do not call the job complete in Vista / billing while open Shelley punch items remain unless the contract allows and the PM documents the exception.

## 3. Typical closeout package (tailor to spec — F-07)

- As-built drawings (redlines)  
- Approved submittals and O&M manuals  
- Test and commissioning reports  
- Panel directories, arc-flash / labels as specified  
- Spare parts / attic stock transmittal  
- Warranty letter (labor/material per contract)  
- Training records if owner training was specified  
- Permit / inspection finals  
- Extra keys, software, passwords for systems Shelley provided  

## 4. Job cost close

Accounting finalizes costs. PM reviews labor and material variance vs estimate and sends notable lessons to estimating (this is continuous improvement).

## 5. Warranty / callback

Treat callbacks as quality events. Log them. Repeat callbacks on the same issue trigger F-05.

## 6. Records

F-06, F-07, transmittals to GC/owner, Vista close date, variance note to estimating.
