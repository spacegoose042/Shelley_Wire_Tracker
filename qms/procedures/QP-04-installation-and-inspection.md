# QP-04 Installation and Inspection

**Shelley Electric, Inc.**  
**Revision:** A (Draft)  
**Related:** T-ITP, T-ITP-26, T-3PH, F-08

## 1. Purpose

Install to the approved documents, the code, and recognized workmanship. Prove it with inspections and tests.

## 2. Standards (typical — confirm per job)

- Contract drawings and specifications  
- NEC (NFPA 70) and local amendments (MABCD / other AHJ)  
- Manufacturer instructions  
- NECA National Electrical Installation Standards (NEIS) as specified or as company workmanship baseline  
- NFPA 72 for fire alarm; NFPA 99 on healthcare jobs; NESC on utility / high voltage work as applicable  
- OSHA 1926 and NFPA 70E / LOTO (safety program)

## 3. Inspection and Test Plan (ITP)

Each major **definable feature of work** gets an ITP (copy T-ITP). Examples: underground, rough-in, gear, lighting, fire alarm, testing.

The ITP lists:

- What is inspected or tested  
- Against what (spec section, drawing, NEIS, manufacturer)  
- Who (crew, GF, GC, owner, AHJ, NETA tech, etc.)  
- Hold / witness points (work does not proceed past a hold without sign-off)

The GF keeps the live ITP on the job. Signed ITPs are scanned to the job file.

## 4. Daily workmanship (internal)

Supervising journeyman / GF checks:

- Correct drawings revision  
- Supports, fill, torque, labeling, firestopping, grounding as that day’s work requires  
- Housekeeping and protection of finished work  

This is not a separate novel form. It is the ITP hold points plus normal supervision.

## 5. External inspections

AHJ, GC, owner, and commissioning agent inspections are logged on F-08 (pass / fail / reinspect).

A failed inspection is an NCR (F-04) unless it is a trivial punch that is corrected the same day and noted on F-08.

**First-pass rate** = passed with no correction ÷ all inspection events.

## 6. Testing

Perform tests required by spec (insulation resistance, continuity, torque, ground resistance, functional, commissioning). Record instrument ID. Keep calibration certificates when required.

Do not energize until required hold points and the GC/owner/AHJ rules for energization are met.

## 7. Healthcare, aviation, and occupied facilities

Add site-specific controls in T-PQP: ICRA, FOD, badging, outage permits, infection control, ESD. Do not bury these only in the spec — put them on the plan the GF sees.

## 8. Records

ITPs, F-08, test reports, photos of covered work (underground, in-wall) before conceal, RFIs affecting installation.
