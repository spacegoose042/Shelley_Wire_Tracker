# QP-01 Contract Review

**Shelley Electric, Inc.**  
**Revision:** A (Draft)  
**Related form:** F-02  
**Related template:** T-PQP

## 1. Purpose

Confirm, before work starts, that we understand the contract, can perform it, and have flowed quality, safety, and specification requirements to the job.

## 2. When

After award (signed contract, purchase order, or notice to proceed) and **before** bulk purchasing and crew mobilization. A lighter F-01 bid review happens before we bid.

## 3. Who

Project manager owns the review. Quality lead reviews if the spec includes a QC plan, ISO, USACE/NAVFAC CQC, healthcare ICRA, or aviation/base requirements. Accounting is notified for Vista setup after review is complete enough to name cost codes and budget.

## 4. Procedure

1. Collect contract, drawings (list revisions), specifications (especially Division 01 and Division 26, plus fire alarm / communications divisions), addenda, bid clarifications, and the estimate turnover notes (assumptions, exclusions, allowances).
2. Complete F-02. Flag:
   - Scope vs estimate mismatches
   - Liquidated damages, schedule milestones, overtime assumptions
   - Required licenses, badging, background checks, drug testing
   - Submittal list and approved-product requirements
   - Testing, commissioning, owner training
   - Closeout documents (O&M, as-builts, warranties, attic stock)
   - Flow-down to vendors and subcontractors
3. If we cannot meet a requirement, raise it in writing (RFI or contract clarification) **before** starting that work.
4. Create or update the project quality plan (T-PQP). Attach ITPs for the main features of work.
5. Request Vista job setup: job/WO number, cost codes aligned to the estimate, budget.
6. File F-02 in the job folder. Brief the general foreman on hold points and approved drawings.

## 5. Records

F-02, T-PQP, ITPs, estimate turnover notes, Vista job number.
