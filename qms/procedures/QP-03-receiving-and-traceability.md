# QP-03 Receiving and Traceability

**Shelley Electric, Inc.**  
**Revision:** A (Draft)  
**Related form:** F-03  
**System:** Wire Tracker (receipts, pulls, returns)

## 1. Purpose

Confirm that material received is the right product, undamaged, and in the right quantity — and that it is tied to the job / work order from receipt through pull and return.

This is the control that already has software behind it. Treat Wire Tracker as a quality record, not only an inventory tool.

## 2. Where material may land

| Path | Who receives | Extra care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company warehouse | Warehouse | Stage by job number. Do not mix jobs on the same reel/spool without a record. |
| Jobsite | GF or designated receiver | Same inspection. Protect from weather, theft, and damage. |

## 3. Receiving inspection (both paths)

Complete F-03 or equivalent Wire Tracker receipt fields:

1. PO / packing slip vs physical goods  
2. Manufacturer and catalog number vs approved submittal (when required)  
3. Quantity and unit (feet vs each — especially wire)  
4. Damage, moisture, missing accessories, cut reels  
5. Heat numbers / reel IDs if the spec or owner requires traceability (healthcare, federal, industrial)

**Fail:** Do not put rejected material into available inventory for that job. Tag it, photograph if significant, notify purchasing and the PM. Open F-04 if it will affect the job.

## 4. Storage

- Follow manufacturer storage (gear upright, wire dry, electronics climate as required).
- Hazardous or high-value gear: locked storage.
- Wire reels labeled with job number and remaining footage after pulls.

## 5. Pulls and returns

- Pulls record user, part, quantity, job name / number, timestamp (Wire Tracker).
- Returns restock to the same job unless the PM releases leftover to stock with a record.
- Do not pull unapproved substitutions.

## 6. Material availability metric

If a crew waits on material, the GF notes the job, duration, and part. Quality lead tallies stockout events monthly (see metrics document). This is the construction version of on-time delivery of material.

## 7. Records

F-03, Wire Tracker transactions, photos of damaged freight, vendor claims.
