## 1. Purpose and scope

This manual describes how Shelley Electric manages quality from bid through closeout.

**In scope:** Electrical construction and related communications / specialty systems performed by Shelley Electric, Inc., including work from the Wichita headquarters and other company locations (for example Emporia, if active). Includes estimating, purchasing, warehouse and jobsite receiving, installation, testing, job costing support, and closeout.

**Out of scope (for now):** Design professional services where Shelley is not the designer of record, unless a design-build contract makes Shelley responsible for design quality. In that case the project quality plan (T-PQP) adds design-review controls.

**Applies to:** Employees, IBEW craft under Shelley supervision, and subcontractors working on Shelley contracts.

## 3. Quality policy and objectives

The signed quality policy is POL-001.

**Standing objectives** (targets set after baseline — see metrics document):

1. Labor hours vs estimate by cost code — improve accuracy and reduce overrun frequency.
2. Inspection first-pass rate — increase.
3. Rework hours as a percent of total labor — decrease.
4. Material availability (crew wait / stockouts) — decrease.
5. Days from substantial completion to closeout — decrease.

Safety metrics (TRIR, EMR) are owned by the safety program and are reported alongside quality at management review because customers require them in prequalification.

## 7. Competence

People who install, supervise, or sign electrical work hold the required trade certificates and, where required, IBEW classification.

The PM maintains a simple competency picture per job: who is the supervising journeyman, who may pull / terminate, who may test, who holds alarm or other specialty credentials.

Training includes toolbox talks, manufacturer training when specified, and KEERC / local CEUs for license renewal.

Apprentices work under journeyman supervision consistent with the CBA and code.

---

## 8. Purchasing, material, and traceability

Material is purchased against the estimate and the approved submittal. The PO references the Vista job / work order number.

Receiving (warehouse or jobsite) confirms identity, quantity, damage, and that the product matches the approved submittal when one exists.

Material is tied to the job in Wire Tracker (receipts, pulls, returns). That record is a quality record.

Unapproved substitutions are nonconformances. They are not “field decisions.”

---

## 9. Production and service provision (the work)

Installation follows:

- Contract documents and approved shop drawings  
- NEC and local amendments  
- Manufacturer instructions  
- Applicable NECA NEIS  
- The project ITP hold points  

Inspection types:

- **Internal:** crew and GF hold points on the ITP  
- **External:** GC, owner, commissioning agent, AHJ  

Failed inspections are nonconformances. First-pass results are logged (F-08).

Testing (megger, torque, continuity, ground, functional tests) is performed as specified. Instruments used for recorded tests are identified; calibration certificates are kept when the spec or the test method requires it.

## 10. Nonconforming outputs

Nonconforming material or work is identified, segregated or marked, and dispositioned: use-as-is (only with written owner/GC/engineer approval), rework, repair, or scrap/return.

See QP-05 and forms F-04 / F-05.
